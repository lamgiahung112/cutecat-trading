Overview

CatCare EA trades gold (XAUUSD) on volume delta: the real balance of buying versus selling pressure inside each candle. Instead of reacting to lagging indicators, it reconstructs the order flow from tick data to see who is actually in control, enters with that pressure (or deliberately against it), and then manages the trade with a stop designed to cut losers quickly and let winners run. No curve-fit neural nets, no black box — just the raw order flow that moves gold.

🎯

One symbol, three independent lanes

CatCare runs three independent lanes side by side from a single XAUUSD chart — each with its own timeframe, direction, threshold and bar count for diversified entries, each holding one position at a time.

The signal — estimated volume delta

Retail gold feeds carry no real, signed volume, so CatCare estimates per-bar delta from raw ticks. Each tick is classified as buyer- or seller-initiated — using the broker's buy/sell tick flags when present, otherwise the tick rule (an uptick is a buy, a downtick a sell, a flat tick carries the prior direction). Every tick counts as ±1.

  • The signal is the rolling sum of per-bar delta over a short window of bars.
  • A lane fires only when the net pressure crosses its threshold — |sum| ≥ Threshold.
  • A lane can run with the pressure, or fade it (trade against the flow), depending on how that lane is configured.
📏

Thresholds are raw tick-delta sums, so they are not comparable across timeframes or brokers. Use the companion Scope indicator to read sane threshold values off your own feed before tuning.

Lanes — three setups in parallel

The EA is organised as independent lanes. Each lane owns its own timeframe, direction, threshold and bar count, and holds one position at a time. The production build runs three fixed lanes together — a diversified blend of timeframes and directions, pre-tuned and locked into the compiled EA.

🔒

The exact per-lane timeframes, directions and thresholds are baked into the production build and aren't published. Use the Scope indicator to read sane threshold values off your own feed when you adjust them.

Each lane carries its own magic number, so the three never clash and you can identify every position by its lane from the order comment (Runner <TF> <buy|sell>).

Exit model — dynamic stop, no fixed take-profit

CatCare uses no take-profit. Winners are given room to develop; the protective stop does the work:

  • Entry stop = the opposite H1 Bollinger band (period 20, deviation 2.0). A BUY stops at the lower band, a SELL at the upper band.
  • A protective fallback stop is used if the band would sit on the wrong side or too close to price.
  • The stop is fixed until the trade is TrailStartPoints in profit, then it trails TrailDistancePoints behind price — only ever tightening, never loosening.

Recovery mode — optional, controlled

Recovery is off by default. When enabled, if a lane's position runs RecoveryPoints against you, the EA adds exactly one same-direction position at 2× the primary's lot, sharing the primary's stop. That is a single, measured add — not an open-ended martingale grid.

  • Only one recovery add per primary cycle; it re-arms when the lane goes flat.
  • Once a lane holds two positions, it switches to basket trailing from the blended break-even, moving both stops together.
  • The shared stop is clamped so it can never lock in a net loss — worst case, the basket exits at break-even.
🔎

With the default RecoveryPoints the band stop is often hit before price moves that far against the trade, so recovery only fires when the band happens to sit wider than that — deliberately conservative.

Lot sizing

Each lane is sized independently, by one of two modes:

Fixed lot

LOT_FIXED — every entry uses the Lots value.

Balance-scaled

LOT_BALANCE_PER_001 — 0.01 lot for every DollarsPer001Lot of account balance, so exposure scales as the account grows.

Scope indicator

CatCare ships with a companion Scope subwindow indicator that plots the same estimated per-bar delta (as a histogram) and the rolling-sum signal line, with the threshold drawn as level lines. Its delta math is identical to the EA's, so you can read sane threshold values off your own broker's feed before you tune a lane.

Requirements

  • Symbol: XAUUSD (gold) only. The signal and exits are built around gold.
  • Account type: Hedging. Several lanes run in parallel and recovery can add a second position on one symbol — a netting account would merge them and break the design.
  • MetaTrader 5. This is an MT5 Expert Advisor.
  • A low-spread broker and a 24/5 VPS are strongly recommended.
  • For backtesting, use "Every tick based on real ticks" — the order-flow signal needs genuine tick history (generated ticks fabricate the delta). Run the tester on a low timeframe so every lane gets ticked.

Installation

  1. Copy the EA into MT5

    In MetaTrader 5 open File → Open Data Folder, then drop CatCare_EA.ex5 into MQL5/Experts/. If you were sent the Scope indicator, drop it into MQL5/Indicators/. Restart MT5 or right-click Navigator → Refresh.

  2. Attach to a XAUUSD chart

    Open a XAUUSD chart (any timeframe — the lanes use their own) and drag CatCare EA onto it. Tick Allow Algo Trading in the dialog, and enable the Algo Trading toolbar button.

  3. Start from the defaults

    Begin with the default settings, then adjust each lane's threshold to match your broker's tick feed using the Scope indicator.

  4. Start on demo

    Run it on a demo account first to confirm behaviour at your broker, then move to a small live account with tiny lots.

Risk warning

⚠️

This EA trades with no take-profit and an optional double-lot recovery add

It carries a real risk of loss, up to and including the entire account. Read this section before using it with real money.

  • No take-profit. Trades are closed by the Bollinger-anchored stop and trail — a sharp reversal can cut a winner back toward break-even.
  • Recovery mode (when enabled) adds a position at twice the lot — a controlled add, but it still increases exposure during an adverse move.
  • Thresholds are feed-specific. A badly tuned threshold can over- or under-trade on your broker; validate with the Scope indicator first.
  • Past performance and backtests do not guarantee future results. Backtest thoroughly on real ticks and run on a demo account first.
  • Use money you can afford to lose, keep the start lot tiny, and size exposure to your balance.
