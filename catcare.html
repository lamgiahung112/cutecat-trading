Overview

CatCare EA trades gold (XAUUSD) with a team of seven engines. Each engine has its own entry logic and looks for a different kind of opportunity: order-flow pressure, session behaviour, the weekly reopen, or gold's relationship with another market. Because the engines rarely trigger at the same moments, together they spread trades out instead of piling them into one setup.

🎯

One chart, seven independent engines

All engines run side by side from a single XAUUSD chart. Each has its own magic number, holds one position at a time, and can be switched on or off on its own. Turning an engine off stops new entries; any position it already holds is still managed to the end.

The engines

Every engine is named after a cat. The entry rules are pre-tuned and locked into the compiled EA; what you control per engine is whether it trades and how its trades are managed.

🐈 Orange Cat
Contrarian order flow

Reads short-term buying and selling pressure from tick data and takes the other side when one side has pushed too hard.

🐆 Prowler
Patient order-flow follower

Waits for sustained buying or selling pressure on a slower timeframe and rides with it.

🐾 Stalker
Order-flow follower

Follows order-flow pressure on a quicker timeframe than Prowler, catching moves earlier.

🧧 Lucky Cat
Asian open momentum

Trades the first push of the Asian trading day, when regional gold demand comes online.

🌙 Night Cat
Overnight to New York

Watches what gold does overnight and carries a strong overnight move into the New York session.

🐯 Pounce
Weekly reopen

Acts when gold reopens for the new week away from Friday's close, trading the follow-through as the market digests weekend news.

🪞 Copycat
Cross-market catch-up

Watches a closely related market. When that market moves and gold lags behind, Copycat trades gold catching up. Off by default.

Order comments name the engine that opened each position, e.g. CatCare LUCKYCAT buy, so you can always see which engine traded.

Exit model: cut losers, let winners run

Every engine manages its trades with the same building blocks, each with its own settings:

  • Protective stop from the start. Every trade opens with a stop placed from the market's recent structure, with a safety fallback if that level isn't usable.
  • Take-profit per engine. Each engine has its own target, set far enough out that winners have room to develop. You can set it to 0 to switch it off.
  • Trailing stop. Once a trade is far enough in profit, the stop starts following price. It only ever tightens, never loosens.
  • Adaptive trailing (optional, per engine). Instead of fixed distances, the trail scales with how volatile gold is when the trade opens: wider in fast markets, tighter in quiet ones. It is on by default for the engines where it works best.

Recovery mode: optional and controlled

Recovery is off by default and set per engine. When enabled, if an engine's position runs a set distance against you, the EA adds exactly one same-direction position at twice the lot, sharing the original stop. It is a single, measured add, not an open-ended martingale grid.

  • Only one recovery add per trade cycle. It re-arms when the engine goes flat.
  • With two positions open, the engine trails them as a basket from their blended break-even, moving both stops together.
  • The shared stop is clamped so it can never lock in a net loss. In the worst case, the basket exits at break-even.

Lot sizing

Every engine sizes its trades with one of two modes:

Fixed lot

LOT_FIXED: every entry uses the Lots value.

Balance-scaled

LOT_BALANCE_PER_001: 0.01 lot for every DollarsPer001Lot of account balance, so exposure grows with the account.

Requirements

  • Symbol: XAUUSD (gold) only. Every engine is built around gold.
  • Account type: Hedging. Several engines can hold positions at once, and recovery can add a second position. A netting account would merge them and break the design.
  • MetaTrader 5. This is an MT5 Expert Advisor.
  • Broker time is detected automatically. The session-based engines work out your broker's server time on their own, including daylight-saving changes. There's nothing to configure.
  • Copycat needs an additional market symbol from your broker. If it isn't available, Copycat switches itself off and says so in the Experts log.
  • A low-spread broker and a 24/5 VPS are strongly recommended.
  • For backtesting, use "Every tick based on real ticks". The order-flow engines need genuine tick history, and generated ticks fabricate it. Start the test a couple of months before the period you want to judge, so every engine has enough history to warm up.

Installation

  1. Copy the EA into MT5

    In MetaTrader 5 open File → Open Data Folder, then drop CatCare_EA.ex5 into MQL5/Experts/. Restart MT5 or right-click Navigator → Refresh.

  2. Attach to a XAUUSD chart

    Open a XAUUSD chart on any timeframe (the engines use their own) and drag CatCare EA onto it. Tick Allow Algo Trading in the dialog, and enable the Algo Trading toolbar button.

  3. Check the Experts log

    On start-up the EA prints a clear message for anything that blocks trading: the Algo Trading button, the EA's own permission, a read-only login, or a broker that doesn't allow automated trading. Fix whatever it reports and the EA starts trading on its own.

  4. Start from the defaults on demo

    The defaults are the tested configuration. Run on a demo account first to confirm behaviour at your broker, then move to a small live account with tiny lots.

Risk warning

⚠️

This EA trades leveraged gold with several engines at once

It carries a real risk of loss, up to and including the entire account. Read this section before using it with real money.

  • Several engines can hold positions at the same time, so total exposure can be a multiple of a single trade.
  • Stops follow the market. In volatile conditions a single loss can be larger than usual, and a sharp reversal can cut a winner back toward break-even.
  • Recovery mode (when enabled) adds a position at twice the lot. It is a controlled add, but it still increases exposure during an adverse move.
  • Past performance and backtests do not guarantee future results. Backtest thoroughly on real ticks and run on a demo account first.
  • Use money you can afford to lose, keep the start lot tiny, and size exposure to your balance.
