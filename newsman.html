Newsman EA

A news-straddle EA that brackets high-impact USD releases — NFP, CPI, FOMC (and optionally PPI) — with stop orders that carry no visible stop-loss, then trails and pyramids the winning side. Driven by the ForexFactory calendar, with reusable profiles, one unified schedule queue, and an optional Telegram control bot.

Overview

Newsman EA trades a news straddle around scheduled, high-impact US-dollar economic releases. A few seconds before a release it places a buy-stop and a sell-stop an equal distance from the current price. Whichever direction the news spikes, that leg fills first and rides the move.

The pending orders deliberately carry no stop-loss while they rest — so a broker can't see the SL and harvest it as liquidity. The instant a leg fills, a protective stop is attached and trailed, and the position can pyramid as price keeps running in your favour.

v2.1 is built around two ideas that run through one shared lifecycle: profiles (named bundles of settings — symbol, lots, distance, SL/TP, trailing, pyramiding) and one queue of scheduled straddles that holds both the automatic ForexFactory events and anything you add by hand. An optional Telegram bot lets you drive the whole thing from your phone.

How it works

1 · Arm before the release

Newsman keeps a schedule of upcoming releases. InpSecondsBefore seconds before an event it drops two pending orders around price:

  • Buy-stop placed InpDistancePoints above the current price.
  • Sell-stop placed InpDistancePoints below the current price.
  • Both rest with no stop-loss attached — the stop is deliberately kept off the order.

Placement and management run on every tick, so the straddle still arms even in a thin, quiet pre-news market rather than waiting on a once-a-second timer.

2 · A leg fills on the spike

When the number drops and price jumps, one leg triggers. At that moment Newsman attaches a protective stop InpInitialSLPoints away and begins trailing it: once the trade is InpTrailActivationPoints in profit, the stop follows price at a distance of InpTrailDistancePoints, re-modifying only when it can improve by at least InpTrailStepPoints. An optional server-side take-profit (InpTakeProfitPoints, 0 = off) can be attached on fill as well.

3 · Pyramiding (optional)

With InpEnablePyramid on, Newsman adds further positions as price runs, spaced InpPyramidStepPoints beyond the furthest open position (one added per tick once price has moved a step past the front), up to InpPyramidMaxPositions per direction. All same-direction positions share one trailed stop, so the whole group is protected together. Because the spacing is measured from live positions, it stays correct after a restart or partial close and never places behind the front runner.

4 · The opposite leg

The unfilled leg is handled one of two ways:

OCO mode

With InpCancelOppositeOnFill on, the opposite pending order is cancelled the moment one leg fills.

Keep-alive mode (default)

Otherwise the unfilled leg stays live for InpKeepOppositeMinutes after the release (to catch a whipsaw reversal), then it's deleted.

🥷

Virtual SL — hide the stop entirely

With InpVirtualSL on, no server-side stop is used at all. The protective/trailed level is held inside the EA's own logic (and mirrored to disk for crash recovery), and when price trades through it the EA closes the whole same-direction group itself. This hides the stop from the broker completely — at the cost of requiring the EA (and your VPS) to stay running to honour it. A take-profit is harmless to rest on the server, so it is still used even in virtual mode.

Event source

🟢 Live trading

Reads the ForexFactory weekly JSON feed. Each event's timestamp carries an explicit UTC offset, which Newsman converts to broker-server time using the measured server↔GMT offset. Only USD · High-impact NFP / CPI / FOMC (and PPI, if enabled) are armed; the full week is kept for the Telegram /news list. The feed refreshes only when the EA is idle (no event window and nothing open).

🧪 Strategy Tester

There is no WebRequest in the tester, so Newsman builds its own schedule: NFP from the first-Friday-of-the-month rule, plus hardcoded CPI / FOMC / PPI release dates (actual historical dates). UTC times are converted to broker-server time using the offset settings below.

🌐

WebRequest must be whitelisted (live)

For live trading, add the feed host to Tools → Options → Expert Advisors → “Allow WebRequest for listed URL”:

  • https://nfs.faireconomy.media — the ForexFactory feed (required).
  • https://api.telegram.org — only if you use the Telegram bot.

Without the whitelist the EA cannot fetch events and has nothing to arm.

Profiles & the queue

Everything Newsman trades runs through one generic lifecycle, keyed by its own settings rather than the chart symbol. Two concepts drive it:

📦 Profile

A named bundle of symbol, magic, lots, distance, SL / TP, trailing and pyramiding. A profile is a position group (a unique symbol + magic), so its trailed anchors live on the profile. Profile #0 is the default: it is re-seeded from the Inp* inputs on every startup, and can be edited live over Telegram. Extra profiles persist to disk.

🗂️ Queue

One list of scheduled straddles. Both the automatic ForexFactory events (→ profile #0) and your Telegram /add jobs live here and are driven identically — only the source (auto vs. manual) differs. Manual jobs persist; auto events are always re-derived from the feed.

Delete an auto event with /del and Newsman remembers it, so the next feed refresh won't re-arm it. Open positions always keep running under their own profile, independent of the queue.

Telegram bot (optional)

A second, independent control channel for live trading. The bot only answers commands — it does not push trade notifications — and it is silent while any position is open, polling for commands only when flat so it never competes with live stop management. Messages from any chat other than your authorized InpTelegramChatId are ignored.

Setup

Create a bot with @BotFather to get a token, set InpTelegramToken and your numeric InpTelegramChatId, enable InpTelegramEnable, and whitelist https://api.telegram.org for WebRequest. The bot is disabled in the Strategy Tester.

Requirements

  • MetaTrader 5 — this is an MT5 Expert Advisor (newsman.ex5).
  • Hedging account. Live trading needs a hedging account — the two-sided straddle and pyramiding both open multiple positions per symbol, which a netting account forbids. On a netting account the EA detects this and disables trading. (The tester is exempt, so you can still backtest on netting.)
  • WebRequest whitelisted for nfs.faireconomy.media (and api.telegram.org if using the bot).
  • A VPS / 24-5 uptime is strongly recommended — especially with Virtual SL, where the EA must be running to honour the stop.
  • Attach it to the chart of the symbol you want to straddle (a major USD pair such as EURUSD or a USD-driven instrument).

Installation

  1. Copy the EA into MT5

    In MetaTrader 5 open File → Open Data Folder, then drop newsman.ex5 into MQL5/Experts/. Restart MT5 or right-click Navigator → Refresh.

  2. Whitelist WebRequest

    Under Tools → Options → Expert Advisors, tick Allow WebRequest for listed URL and add https://nfs.faireconomy.media (and https://api.telegram.org for the Telegram bot).

  3. Enable algo trading

    Turn on the Algo Trading toolbar button so the EA can place orders.

  4. Attach to a chart

    Drag Newsman EA onto the chart of your chosen symbol. In the dialog, tick Allow Algo Trading.

  5. Set your inputs

    Choose which events to trade, your lot size, straddle distance and risk/trailing settings. Defaults are listed in the inputs reference below.

  6. Confirm it armed

    Check the Experts journal — Newsman logs the feed fetch and each event it arms (e.g. armed NFP @ … (server)). A smiley face on the chart means it's running.

Risk warning

⚠️

Read before you trade real money

Newsman trades around the most volatile moments in the market. News spikes bring slippage, gapping and wild spreads — a stop can fill far from its level, and pyramided positions amplify both wins and losses.

  • Slippage & spread blow-outs around releases can fill orders well away from your intended price.
  • Pyramiding increases exposure fast — many positions can be open in one direction at once.
  • Virtual SL depends on uptime. If the EA, terminal or VPS goes down, the hidden stop is not enforced.
  • Backtests are illustrative only and use a fixed historical event schedule, not a live feed. They do not guarantee future results.
  • Start on a demo account, keep lots tiny, and only trade money you can afford to lose.

Trading leveraged products is high-risk and may not be suitable for everyone. You use this EA entirely at your own risk; the author accepts no liability for any losses.
